Dear Alina,

Happy birthday. 🤍

I could have written you a normal birthday message, added a few emojis, wished you happiness and called it a day.

But somehow that felt too ordinary.

So I made this little place instead.

It took time, a lot of tiny details, a questionable amount of staring at code, and probably more effort than a birthday website has any right to require.

But honestly, that was the fun part.

I wanted you to open this and slowly discover things. The photographs. The flowers. The ridiculous fireworks. The tiny interactions. The music.

Not because everything has to be perfect, but because sometimes making something for someone is its own kind of happiness.

I hope 21 is kind to you.

I hope you get more moments that make you genuinely happy, more people who make you feel appreciated, more adventures you didn't plan for, and more ordinary days that somehow turn into your favourite memories.

And whenever life gets busy, I hope you still remember to stop sometimes and enjoy the small things.

Anyway... enough serious words.

Today you're officially 21, so please go enjoy your birthday properly. 😂

I really hope this little surprise made you smile.

— from someone who spent far too long making this ✦
